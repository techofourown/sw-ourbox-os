The key words **SHALL**, **SHALL NOT**, **SHOULD**, **SHOULD NOT**, and **MAY** are to be interpreted as requirements.

OurBox OS vocabulary and normative definitions are defined in `docs/00-Glossary/Terms-and-Definitions.md`.
