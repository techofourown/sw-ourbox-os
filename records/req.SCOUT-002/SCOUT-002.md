Scout SHALL accept user-provided civic artifacts for analysis.

At minimum, Scout SHALL accept:

* URL inputs,
* text/HTML inputs,
* image artifacts, and
* PDF artifacts.

Examples include political mailers, campaign flyers, candidate webpages, public notices, and issue handouts.
