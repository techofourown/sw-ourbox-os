# ADR-0002

Local replicas are per tenant origin and shared across apps within that origin.

Tenant-origin patterns:
- `http://<tenant_id>.local/...`
- `https://<tenant_id>.<box-host>/...`

Same tenant across those two origins is origin-split and local-replica-split.

Replication remains same-origin via `/db` in both modes.
